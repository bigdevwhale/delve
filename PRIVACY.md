# Privacy

Delve has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your hero, loot, camp and chronicle stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

**Location.** The dungeon is built from the streets around you, and the hero walks where you walk. Your position is used on the phone only and is never sent anywhere. While an expedition is on, a service with a quiet notification keeps listening to the GPS, so the walk goes on with the phone in a pocket. The routes of past expeditions are kept on the phone for the chronicle.

**The street map** is downloaded from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap vector tiles): only the tiles covering the district box are requested. The demo build carries its map inside the app.

**The weather** comes from MET Norway: only the camp's coordinates, rounded to about a kilometre, are sent.

**Links in the settings** (report a bug, suggest an idea, follow updates, the StillCraft chat and the support page) open in your browser or Telegram only when you tap them.
