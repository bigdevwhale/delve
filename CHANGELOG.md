## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

First release.

- A dungeon under your district built from OpenStreetMap streets: corridors, halls at crossings, cave-ins, stairs down; drawn like an old hand-inked map.
- Places above shape the rooms below: crypts under churches, springs under fountains, grottoes under parks, rivers by water, merchants under shops.
- The torch is the walk's length; creatures can be fought in short rounds or walked off in a chase; buzzes in the pocket.
- Depth stake, deep camps after floor bosses, daily guild orders, 4 classes up to level 20, gear with rarities, six camp buildings, 80 creatures to meet and tame.
- GPS filter from Fogwalk; English and Russian.
